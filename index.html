This application is created by Avadhesh Khandelwal, who is also a student, built through more than 15 days of continuous dedication and hard work.

How to Use ArckType
1. Pick Your Drill

Launch an instant 1-minute test, tackle a 100-word marathon, or practice specialized symbol, punctuation, and custom passages.

2. Configure Arena

Adjust rolling lines (1–10), cursor type (bar or arrow), and backspace rules (Off for strict exam simulations).

3. Screen vs Paper Mode

Use Screen Mode for split-view typing feedback, or Paper Mode for strict blind-exam source text practice.

4. Fluid Space & Skip

Press Space to advance words. If you skip a word and type the next one, the app automatically resyncs your pace.

5. Detailed Review

Check your exact WPM and accuracy tiers on the scorecard, view mistyped letters/words, or click Review in History to revisit past tests.

6. 3 Student Profiles

Open Settings to switch between 3 independent student accounts, custom fonts, button shapes, and 18 landscape themes.
